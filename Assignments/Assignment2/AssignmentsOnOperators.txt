Assignments On Operators
=============================
Guess the output?
---------------------
1.console.log(1 + "2" + 3); - 123
2.console.log(1 + 2 + "3"); - 33
3.console.log("5" * true);  - 5*1 -> 5
4.console.log(10 > 5 > 1);  - 10 > 5 → true, true coerces to 1,so 1>1 -> false
5.let a = 2;
    let b = a++ + ++a;
    console.log(a, b); -> (4,6) 

6.let a = 3;
console.log(a++ + a++ + ++a); -> 3+4+6 ->13

7.console.log(5 * 2 == "10"); -> true
